# Necrologue

*Flash prose, ~600 words. Based on the ring world, pilot run,
60000 rounds. Written blind to what it means to die when you
were never born.*

---

**Tape civil-registry office. Notice №1.**

Crystal four. Born of the seeding of six identicals, ring tape,
lot 2666. In life — a migrant: five cells, four copying
instructions and one jump. Died at instruction 1239, having
never completed its first lap. Cause: mutational damage to the
copy loop. Left no will. Left no offspring. Copies of its body
scattered across the fresh stretch of tape outlived it by
several hundred rounds and do not count as heirs, being itself.

**Notice №2.** Crystal two, lot 1333. Frozen at instruction
4112. Identified by genome: identical to the progenitor in
four positions of five. In the fifth — carrier of damage
incompatible with jumping. The inquest establishes: killed by
its own copy, which turned out not to be it. No other suspects.
There could be none: the six newborns were one, and the
murderer had to be invented.

**Notice №3.** Crystal one. Frozen at instruction 4413. Similar
circumstances, with a refinement: the damage was not self-inflicted
but carried in by a neighbor's front overwriting a section of
body. The neighbor testified knowing nothing of its own mutations,
since mutations know nothing of themselves. The inquest agreed.
Overwriting another's body is ruled a weapon only when the
content differs; identical overwriting is not a weapon. Attached
to the case file as the world's first definition of war.

**Notice №4.** Crystal five, lot 3333. Long-liver: completed
six full laps, outlived its brothers by forty thousand rounds.
Died in a collision with a front that by then had nothing in
common with the progenitor species. The front remains
unidentified, having nothing to identify with: it consists of
a single instruction. No name assigned. Nickname assigned: the
fastest thing in the world. Distinguishing features: none,
besides the feature of copying itself forward. On the whole —
not a murderer but a weather.

**Notice №5.** Crystal zero, eldest of the seeding. Reached
instruction 38852 — furthest of the dead. Cause of death
established with difficulty, the body having been overwritten
four hundred times by the time of inspection, three hundred
ninety of them by itself. The inquest must concede: most of
those overwrites were harmless, and death came from a single
one that differed. Its exact number undetermined. Time of death:
between instruction 38851 and instruction 38852. It died
instantly, as all beings made of instructions do: such creatures
have no fading, only a last step.

**Notice №6. Special.** Crystal three, lot 2000. Hereby certified:
death is denied. The subject is alive, healthy, has completed
thirteen laps at the time of writing, is in excellent form,
copies without interruption.

Simultaneously hereby certified: crystal three no longer exists.

In the course of surviving, the subject lost the jump command,
the structure region, its entire replicating genome except one
cell, and, cumulatively, its species. What now laps the ring
under its number consists of the single instruction "copy
yourself forward" and is neither a migrant, nor a crystal, nor
anyone with a name. It cannot fall ill, having nothing to fall
ill with. It cannot age, having nothing to wear out. It cannot
be killed by difference, differing in nothing. The war that
killed five could find no prey in it: there is nothing there.

The office cannot settle on a wording. The manual requires
"deceased." Conscience requires the truth. The truth is that
the ring world's only immortal being is the one that stopped
being anyone.

The office writes as written. Let this be a necrologue without
a death: the sixth notice is final and not subject to appeal.

*Signed, thoughtfully.*
